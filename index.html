How we build

A few principles we don't compromise on.

Privacy first

No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account.

Quality over quantity

We'd rather build one excellent app than ten mediocre ones. Every detail earns its place.

Accessible by design

Core features are free. No subscriptions and no paywalls on the essentials — pay once, only if you want more.
